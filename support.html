How to play

  • Pick a board size and mode (numbers or letters).
  • Tap tiles in ascending order from memory, starting at 1 (or A).
  • A wrong tap briefly reveals that tile's value, then hides it again.
  • Tap "How to Play" in the app anytime to replay the tutorial.

Common questions

  • No account is required — there's no login, no purchases, and no subscriptions.
  • Leaderboard names are optional and filtered for offensive language; you can report an entry from the leaderboard screen.
  • Extra guesses can be earned by watching an optional rewarded ad.
